Questions, answered

When does the EU Battery Passport become mandatory?

18 February 2027 for EV, LMT and industrial batteries above 2 kWh (Regulation 2023/1542). The registry went live 20 July 2026; implementation guidance v2.0 (71 data points) published August 2026.

What data goes in the battery passport?

71 points across identity, capacity, exhaustion threshold, internal resistance, C-rate, carbon footprint, safety, test reports, SoH + cycles + durability class. This tool maps 9 rows today (mapped/partial/operator-attach) — see DPP_MAP in js/database.js.

Does a used EV need a passport at resale?

From Feb 2027, in-scope batteries placed on the EU market need a QR-linked passport; resellers should carry SoH + cycles documentation now. The free cert QR (BATTCERTv2 + DPPpre JSON) is forward-compatible evidence.

How do I generate a pre-DPP QR free?

Run the forensic → Resale tab → the tool emits BATTCERTv2 pipe payload + DPPpre JSON (SoH ± band, EFC, cycles left, trigger, VIN-hash, method, timestamp, SHA-256). Print at 25 mm for listings.

What is the 'exhaustion threshold' (no universal 80%)?

Correct — there is no single 80% rule; the threshold is per-application. The tool maps YOUR OEM warranty trigger (70%, Leaf 66.25% bars, Bolt 60%) as the exhaustion basis.

Who attaches the carbon footprint?

The operator (manufacturer/importer) via a PEF study — the tool marks this operator-attach and anchors pricing context to BNEF Dec-2025. It does not invent carbon numbers.

India/SEA sellers: why care about EU DPP?

EU-compliant documentation raises export and fleet-buyer trust, and the same QR converts on OLX/Cars24/CarMax today. Compliance tailwind, not hype.
