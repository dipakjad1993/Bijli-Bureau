What QR spec does the pre-passport payload follow?

BATTCERTv3 resolves as https://batteryforensics.in/cert/#{uid} (registry-resolved pattern). Identifier: EN 18219:2026 or equivalent (ISO/IEC 15459 / GS1). Carrier: EN 18220:2026 or equivalent. Access tiers: public (SoH, SOCE, cycles, trigger), legitimate-interest (VIN-hash, delta-V, service refs), authority (full VIN, raw telemetry). Pre-passport credential — NOT an Art.77 passport.

Questions, answered

When does the EU Battery Passport become mandatory?

18 February 2027 for EV, LMT and industrial batteries above 2 kWh (Regulation 2023/1542 Art.77(1), unchanged by Omnibus IV). The registry went live 20 July 2026 (IR 2026/1778); Guidance v2.0 (71 data points) 15 Aug 2026; only ~46–47 mandatory for EV at Feb-2027, rest deferred/conditional.

What data goes in the battery passport?

71 points: identity, capacity, exhaustion threshold (no universal 80%), SOCE + capacity fade + resistance increase + lifecycle status (mandatory EV), carbon (deferred), recycled content (from Aug-2028), safety, test reports, operator chain. Full flagged table below — each row mandatory@2027 / conditional / deferred / na-for-EV.

Does a used EV need a passport at resale?

From Feb 2027, in-scope batteries placed on the EU market need a QR-linked passport; resellers should carry SoH + cycles + SOCE documentation now. The free cert QR (BATTCERTv3 + DPPpre JSON) is forward-compatible evidence — explicitly a pre-passport credential, not an Art.77 passport.

How do I generate a pre-DPP QR free?

Run the forensic → Resale tab → the tool emits BATTCERTv3 pipe payload + DPPpre JSON (SoH ± band, SOCE kWh, EFC, cycles left, lifecycle status, trigger, VIN-hash, access tier, method, timestamp, SHA-256). Print at 25 mm for listings. QR aligns to EN 18220 / identifier EN 18219 or equivalent.

What is the 'exhaustion threshold' (no universal 80%)?

Correct — there is no single 80% rule; the threshold is per-application. The tool maps YOUR OEM warranty trigger (70%, Leaf 66.25% bars, Bolt 60%) as the exhaustion basis.

Who attaches the carbon footprint?

The operator (manufacturer/importer) via a PEF study — deferred until Art.7 acts adopt. The tool marks this operator-attach and anchors pricing context to the BNEF Dec-2025 snapshot. It does not invent carbon numbers.

Who can see my battery SoH under the access tiers?

Pending the Art.77(9) access-rights act (missed 18 Aug 2026; draft Oct–Nov 2026, adoption Q4 2026). This tool structures payloads as public / legitimate-interest / authority tiers now, so compliance flips a config flag, not a schema. SoH sits in the restricted tier.

India/SEA sellers: why care about EU DPP?

EU-compliant documentation raises export and fleet-buyer trust, and the same QR converts on OLX/Cars24/CarMax today. Compliance tailwind, not hype.
