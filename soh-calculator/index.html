Questions, answered

How is EV battery SoH calculated without a lab?

EFC (odometer ÷ certified range, sublinear EFC^0.85) for cycle fade + plateaued Arrhenius calendar aging (city pack temperature, chemistry Ea) + C-rate/SoC stress, fused with charge-log/OBD evidence when provided. Uncertainty band = Monte Carlo sampling plus a per-chemistry field-variance floor — composition disclosed per run.

Why do most SoH calculators disagree with the dealer BMS readout?

They divide real-world km by certified range (bakes ~20% efficiency loss in as fake degradation), compound calendar aging linearly forever, and show point estimates with no band. This tool calibrates CSVs to the driver's own baseline, plateaus calendar after year 2–3, and publishes residuals vs AVILOO (mean 1.5–1.8 pts).

What inputs move SoH most?

Age × city heat (Chennai ≈1.9× Pune at 100% SoC high-Ni), DC-fast share (NMC punished, LFP tolerant), habitual parking SoC, and total km (sublinear). Run the free tool with your numbers.

Is a photo-only SoH enough to file a warranty claim?

It is supporting evidence, never the determination. File with the evidence PDF + demand the OEM BMS SoH % and threshold in writing. The claim letter template cites your exact clause.

How often should I re-check SoH?

Every 60–90 days once within 5% of your warranty trigger; annually otherwise. The trajectory fan shows your crossing year ± band.

Does live weather change my SoH?

Yes, boundedly: live ambient nudges pack temperature ±1.75°C max; IMD normals remain the anchor. Offline, normals are used with a snapshot label.

Which chemistries hold SoH best in India heat?

LFP/LMFP and sodium-ion (tolerant, flat curves); high-Ni NMC/NCA need strict 20–80% bands and shade parking. See the chemistry fingerprints in the tool.
