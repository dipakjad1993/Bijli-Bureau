Questions, answered

How much does battery certification add to resale?

Model: certified ask = base × grade multiplier (A+ 1.06 … D 0.78) with +9% cert premium vs uncertified (×0.93); dealer bid ≈0.88× certified. AVILOO's 13.5% intra-model spread implies up to ₹60k (Ola-class) / $5k (Model-3-class) swings on identical listings (illustrative).

What does the buyer QR show?

BATTCERTv2: brand/model, SoH ± band, grade, range today/new, odometer, city, date, EFC, evidence source, VIN-hash — plus DPPpre JSON. Scans to verifiable text even before any registry exists.

How is the price band computed?

Age depreciation (13%/yr 2W, 11%/yr 4W) × km factor × battery-share factor (42% of EV value is battery, health-scaled) × grade multiplier. Every factor is shown in Tab 5 — no black box.

What should I paste into my OLX/Cars24 listing?

The one-click price band + cert text buttons copy: band, grade, SoH ± band, dealer-bid expectation, and the QR payload. Stick the printed QR (25 mm) on the listing photos.

Do dealers (Cars24/CarMax/OLX) accept this cert?

It is third-party supporting evidence with method + timestamp + hash — present it alongside the OEM BMS printout you demand in writing. Certified packs report +12% margin, ~22 days faster turn (2025 dealer self-reported — directional).

What grade is my battery?

A+ ≥93 · A ≥88 · B+ ≥80 · B ≥72 · C ≥65 · D below. Grade + plain-language note (e.g. 'Tired — price guard + warranty watch') print on the health card.

Can a certificate be faked?

Ours carries method + CI + timestamp + SHA-256 over the payload — a buyer can re-hash. Owner attests odo/photos genuine; fraud is on the attester, detectable on re-test.

Does this help lenders/insurers?

Yes: auditable SoH ± band + cycles + hash is risk-pricing input. API shape documented in README (POST /v1/forensic → {soh, ci, grade, warranty, qr, hash}).
