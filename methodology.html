By BijliBureau · Not legal advice. Always demand OEM BMS SoH in writing and verify your booklet year. Supporting evidence only. Reproduce everything: node tools/backtest.js (ships in repo).

1 · SoH Lab — odometer-anchored sublinear EFC + plateaued Arrhenius + C-rate + driver-baseline fusion + honest band

EFC = odo_km / certified_range_new_km (ODOMETER-ANCHORED; CSV never extrapolates EFC)
cycleFade%  = EFC^0.85 × fadePerEFC(chemistry) × DoDstress × CrateStress
  fadePerEFC = 20/cycleLife ENFORCED (LFP 4000→0.0050 · NMC622 1800→0.0111 · NMC811 1500→0.0133
  NCA 1600→0.0125 · LMO 1200→0.0167 · LMFP 3000→0.0067 · Na-ion 2500→0.0080 · LTO 12000→0.0017
  NMC9-Si 1300→0.0154 · Semi-solid 1600→0.0125 · Na-G2 3000→0.0067)
  DoDstress   = 0.80 + depthAvg/250 (≈0.88 @20% … 1.20 @100%)
  CrateStress = 1 + fastPct×fastPenalty×0.55 + ultra8C_flag×0.35
calendarFade% = effAge × baseRate(chemistry) × Arrhenius(Tpack, Ea) × SoCstress
  effAge = age≤2 ? age : 2+(age−2)^0.70  (packs flatten after year 2–3; linear compounding removed v2026.14)
  Arrhenius = exp[(Ea/R)(1/298.15 − 1/Tpack)] · R=8.314 J/mol·K · Ea 22–43 kJ/mol by chemistry
  Tpack = IMD pack-effective temp, nudged by LIVE ambient ±1.75°C max when online (IMD stays anchor)
  SoCstress = 0.75 + 0.0125×(avgSoC−50), ×1.15 if parked >80%, capped 1.7
CSV health vs DRIVER BASELINE (median implied range of earliest full charges — NOT certified range),
  recency decay exp(−staleDays/540) a year-old log keeps ~44% weight, and the UI says which baseline was used.
Bayesian fuse: w_csv = min(0.40, rows/30 × days/365) × decay; dash-range w=0.15; OBD BMS w=min(0.80, 0.5+n/200)
SoH_raw kept + SoH clamped 45–100 (display floor 45; band floor 40 — documented, consistent).
90% UNCERTAINTY BAND (honest label, v2026.14): band = √(MC² + fieldFloor²), n=600
  MC: odo ±2%, Tpack ±1.5°C, age ±15 DAYS (all really sampled) + CSV ±1.0% / OBD ±0.5% observation noise
  fieldFloor per chemistry (AVILOO spread ladder): LFP 1.6 · NMC622 2.2 · NMC811 2.4 · NCA 2.2 · LMO 2.6
    LMFP 1.8 · Na-ion 1.7 · LTO 1.2 · high-Ni 2.4 · semi-solid 2.3, × (1+age/24)
  evidence caps: solid CSV ≤±3.0% · strong OBD ≤±1.8%
  What this means: model-only LFP ≈±1.8% young → 12-yr NMC ≈±3.7%. The OLD "±3.5/2.8/1.5 marketing
  tiers" are gone; the band now MOVES with evidence (verified: OBD-strong 1.8 < model-only 2.8).
OBD: cell delta-V rounded BEFORE compare (v2026.12 epsilon fix); >150mV WARN, >250mV CRITICAL; BMS fused second.
Dashboard SoC-now is EVIDENCE CONTEXT (photo record + fingerprint flag), never a silent model lever.

Chemistry table: LFP 4000 cycles / 1.1%/yr / Ea 28kJ · NMC622 1800/1.6%/38kJ · NMC811 1500/1.9%/42kJ · NCA 1600/1.8%/40kJ · LMO 1200/2.2%/35kJ · LMFP 3000/1.25%/30kJ · Na-ion 2500/1.3%/26kJ · LTO 12000/0.5%/22kJ · NMC9-Si 1300/2.0%/43kJ · Semi-solid 1600/1.7%/39kJ · Na-G2 3000/1.15%/25kJ. Pack-effective temp = IMD mean + garage/charging soak (e.g. Chennai 29.6→36.2°C, Pune 24.7→29.8°C). Heat gap = pure SoC-amplified Arrhenius ratio vs Pune (≈1.38× @78% SoC LFP Chennai; max ≈1.9× @100% SoC high-Ea NMC; Dubai ≈2.0× — computed, §10).

10 · Heat-gap note (computed, not claimed)

hotGapFactor = Arr(city)/Arr(Pune) × (1+max(0,avgSoC−70)×0.0117). Chennai vs Pune: ≈1.38× @78% SoC LFP (the demo number), max ≈1.9× @100% SoC high-Ea NMC. Dubai vs Pune ≈2.0×. The old "~2.1×" line overstated by ~0.2 and is corrected everywhere. Shade-parking + 80% cap recovers ~40% of the gap.

15 · California CARB MY2026+ SoH standardization (13 CCR §§1962.5/1962.7/1962.8 + DMV Jan-2027)

SoH (State of Health, CARB definition)
Normalized usable battery energy today as 0–100%, reported via standardized parameters. California requires reported SoH be no more than 5 percentage points HIGHER than measured usable energy.
EFC (Equivalent Full Cycles)
Total km driven divided by certified range when new. Odometer-anchored here; never extrapolated from charge logs.
Arrhenius calendar aging
Heat-driven fade: exp[(Ea/R)(1/298.15 − 1/Tpack)]. Plain English: a hot-parked pack ages faster than a cool-parked pack, scaled by chemistry.
SOCE (State of Certified Energy)
Certified usable energy in kWh — mandatory EV passport field at Feb-2027. This tool prints SOCE kWh = rated kWh × SoH on every run.

13 CCR §1962.5(c)(4)(A)4.c: MY2026+ ZEVs/PHEVs must report normalized SoH with accuracy such that reported SoH is no more than 5pp HIGHER than measured usable energy. Dashboard-displayable (§1962.5(c)(6)). §1962.8 warranty: SoH below 70% at 8yr/100k mi (MY2026–30), below 75% from MY2031. §1962.7 enforcement: CARB sample-tests; recall if over 30% sampled report over 8pp high (MY26–28) / 5pp high (MY29+). CA DMV used-EV disclosure (Jan 2027): dealers disclose SoH% or est. range, last OTA affecting BMS/range, battery replaced/repaired + date, remaining warranty + transferability, open HV recalls; third-party diagnostics allowed if DMV-certified; 3-yr retention, audits, fines.

What SoH accuracy does California require from MY2026 EVs?

Within 5 percentage points high (13 CCR §1962.5): reported SoH must be no more than 5pp above measured usable energy, shown on the dash. This tool's band (±1.8 OBD-strong → ±3.7 old-NMC model-only) sits INSIDE that ceiling — see CARB explainer + comparison.
