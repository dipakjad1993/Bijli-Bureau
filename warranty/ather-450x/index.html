What is the Ather 450X HR (3.7 kWh) battery warranty trigger in 2026?

The Ather 450X HR (3.7 kWh) high-voltage battery is covered when measured State of Health falls below 70%. Term: 5 years / 60,000 km, whichever hits first. Remedy and threshold follow Ather Battery Protect §2.1 — 70% capacity assurance. Always confirm your booklet year, because mid-cycle OEM updates change terms.

How long does the 450X HR (3.7 kWh) battery warranty last?

5 years or 60,000 km, whichever hits first. Ather Battery Protect 5yr/60k; extended to 8yr/80k on Rizta/2024+ 450 (Battery Protect 8). <70% triggers. File while both time and kilometre limits are alive — whichever hits first ends coverage. Keep service stamps clean.

What are the top 3 denial grounds for a 450X HR (3.7 kWh) battery claim?

The three most common denial grounds are: (1) Requires scheduled service at Ather Grid SC; deep-discharge storage >30 days excluded; (2) missing or irregular OEM service history; (3) booklet-year mismatch (pre-update vs post-update stock). Counter with BMS SoH in writing, stamped history, and RC-year proof.
