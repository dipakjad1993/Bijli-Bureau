What is the River Indie (4 kWh) battery warranty trigger in 2026?

The River Indie (4 kWh) high-voltage battery is covered when measured State of Health falls below 70%. Term: 5 years / 80,000 km, whichever hits first. Remedy and threshold follow River warranty — 70%. Always confirm your booklet year, because mid-cycle OEM updates change terms.

How long does the Indie (4 kWh) battery warranty last?

5 years or 80,000 km, whichever hits first. 5yr/80k. File while both time and kilometre limits are alive — whichever hits first ends coverage. Keep service stamps clean.

What are the top 3 denial grounds for a Indie (4 kWh) battery claim?

The three most common denial grounds are: (1) Standard; (2) missing or irregular OEM service history; (3) booklet-year mismatch (pre-update vs post-update stock). Counter with BMS SoH in writing, stamped history, and RC-year proof.
