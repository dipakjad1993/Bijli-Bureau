What is a used EV battery worth per kWh in 2026?

Route pricing used here: home backup ≈$88/kWh, fleet/e-rickshaw re-pack ≈$58, certified recycler floor ≈$26 (WoodMac H1-2026 ESS-boom context), × 0.72 install margin minus refurb share ($320 2W / $900 4W × 0.35). Packs above 70% SoH earn reuse premium; 50–70% ESS-grade; below 50% recycle floor.

Why does new-cell pricing cap second-life value?

Record-low new cells (BNEF Dec-2025: pack $108/kWh, LFP $81) cap reuse at ~60–80% of new-pack value. This market moves ~40% CAGR — re-verify quarterly; the tool states its anchor date on every figure.

LFP vs NMC for second life?

LFP is ideal for stationary ESS (tolerant, safe, flat curve); NMC accepted with BMS. High-Ni packs with heavy fast-charge history grade lower — the chemistry fingerprint flags it.
