Questions, answered

What is the Real-World SoH Index?

A quarterly publication (PDF + landing table) of field SoH by age bucket, chemistry, city-climate bucket and fast-charge share — from opt-in anonymized runs only. n, mix and method printed every quarter.

What data leaves my device?

Only city-climate bucket + chemistry + EFC bucket + SoH bucket + app version + date. No VIN, no raw odometer, no device ID, no photos. Consent checkbox gates a 200-cap revocable outbox; POST only when a telemetry endpoint is configured.

Where is the anonymization spec?

Methodology §4 + tools/soh-index-worker.js (Cloudflare Worker + R2 reference). Buckets: city→climate zone, odo→EFC decile, SoH→1pp bucket. k-anonymity floor: buckets with n<5 are suppressed.

When is the first release?

Q1 after the telemetry endpoint is configured. Until then the outbox queues locally and this page shows the method + empty tables — honestly, not with invented numbers.
