Method statement (printed in PDF)

      
EFC = odo_km / certified_range_new_km (odometer-anchored; CSV used for health only)
cycleFade%  = EFC^0.85 × (20/cycleLife) × DoDstress × CrateStress (sublinear throughput, v2026.15)
calendarFade% = plateaued eff-age × baseRate(chemistry) × Arrhenius(Tpack, Ea) × SoCstress
  eff-age = age≤2 ? age : 2+(age−2)^0.70 · live ambient nudges Tpack ±1.75°C max (IMD anchor kept)
Arrhenius = exp[(Ea/R)(1/298.15 − 1/Tpack)] · R=8.314 J/mol·K
CSV health vs DRIVER baseline (earliest full charges), recency decay exp(−staleDays/540)
Bayesian fuse: w_csv ≤ 0.40 (decayed), dash-range w=0.15; OBD BMS w=min(0.80, 0.5+n/200)
SoH_raw kept + SoH clamped 45–100; 90% band = √(MC² + chemistry field-floor²), n=600
  (odo ±2%, T ±1.5°C, age ±15d jitter — all really sampled; floor per chemistry, grows 1+age/24)
OBD: cell delta-V rounded BEFORE compare; >150mV WARN, >250mV CRITICAL; BMS fused second.
Thresholds per OEM rulebook (default 70%; Leaf ≈66.25% bars; Bolt ≈60%).
