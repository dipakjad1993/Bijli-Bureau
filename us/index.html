US questions, answered

Does this free tool work for US EVs?

Yes — Tesla Model 3/Y, Leaf (66.25% bars trigger), Bolt (~60% trigger), Mach-E, ID.4, Ioniq 5, Polestar 2 and more, with USD pricing and mile-based terms (8yr/100–120k mi). Recurrent is US-only paid telematics; AVILOO is EU/US lab-based; this is free and on-device.

Why is the Leaf trigger 66.25% and not 70%?

Nissan warrants against loss of the 4th capacity bar ≈66.25% SoH (8yr/100k mi). Dealers misquote 70% — bring a LeafSpy cell-voltage printout.

Why is the Bolt trigger ~60%?

GM's propulsion-power trigger sits near 60% (verify your booklet year; recall-fire packs were replaced regardless). Lower than the 70% default — know your clause.

How do US tax credits interact with battery health?

Used-EV credit eligibility is price/age-gated, but value still hinges on SoH: AVILOO's 13.5% spread implies ~$5k swings on Model-3-class listings. Certify with the QR + PDF.

Tesla LFP vs NMC: what changes?

LFP (RWD) tolerates 100% charging and heat; high-Ni NMC (LR/Performance) needs 20–80% discipline. The chemistry fingerprint detects the pattern from your habits + OBD.

Is my data uploaded?

No — photos/CSV/OBD parse in-browser; PDF generates locally. Opt-in telemetry only, revocable.

What accuracy should I expect?

Model-only band ≈±2–4% growing with age (disclosed composition), ≤±3.0% with a solid charge log, ≤±1.8% with strong OBD. Backtest residuals published (mean 1.5–1.8 pts vs AVILOO).
