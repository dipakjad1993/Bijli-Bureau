What does 13 CCR §1962.5 require in one paragraph?

MY2026+ ZEVs/PHEVs sold in California must report normalized battery SoH (0–100%) via standardized parameters, accurate so reported SoH is no more than 5pp higher than measured usable energy, and displayable on the dash. Warranty and enforcement follow §§1962.7–1962.8 above.

Questions, answered

What SoH accuracy does California require from MY2026 EVs?

Within 5 percentage points high: reported SoH must be no more than 5pp above measured usable battery energy (13 CCR §1962.5(c)(4)(A)4.c), displayable to the driver (§1962.5(c)(6)) — no scan tool required.

What is the California battery warranty from MY2026?

SoH below 70% at 8yr/100k mi (MY2026–2030); below 75% from MY2031 (13 CCR §1962.8). In-use enforcement (§1962.7): CARB can sample-test; recall if >30% sampled report >8pp high (MY26–28) / >5pp high (MY29+).

What must California dealers disclose on used EVs from Jan 2027?

SoH% or estimated remaining range, last OTA version affecting BMS/range, battery replaced/repaired status + date, remaining warranty + transferability, open HV recalls. Third-party diagnostics allowed if DMV-certified; 3-year retention, random audits, fines.
