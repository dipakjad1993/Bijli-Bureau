Terms: free tool, honest numbers, your inputs are your attestation

What this tool is

Free, on-device EV battery forensics: SoH estimate ± disclosed band, clause-level warranty verdict, second-life/resale math, timestamped + SHA-256 hashed evidence PDF (optional detached signature when a dealer key is configured). Zero npm dependencies at runtime for the core engine.

What this tool is not

Supporting evidence only — not legal advice, not a BMS readout, not an EU Art.77 Battery Passport. Warranty text on every page is a paraphrase — verify against your owner booklet year + RC year + service stamps. Always demand the OEM BMS SoH percentage and threshold in writing. India escalation notes (CPA 2019 → e-daakhil, helpline 1915) and EU MVBER notes are procedural information, not counsel.

Acceptable use

Do not submit another person's VIN/photos without consent; do not forge odometer or service inputs (owner attests inputs genuine — fraud is on the attester and detectable on re-test); do not misrepresent the evidence PDF as an OEM or government certificate. Dealers: embed mode ?embed=1 is allowed with attribution + linkback; server-side verdict API requires a key (see api/openapi.json).

Liability + corrections

Method + residuals published (Methodology §6); model-only SoH carries a ±1.8–3.7% band that moves with evidence. Report errors via GitHub Issues — 7-day fix SLA (corrections log).
